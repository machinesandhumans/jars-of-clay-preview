Strengthen Local Churches in Malawi

Your partnership helps train local pastors in sound theology, practical agriculture, and entrepreneurial skills—while providing the ongoing mentorship and accountability they need to lead healthy churches and build sustainable ministries in their communities.

Rather than creating long-term dependence on outside support, Jars of Clay Foundation – Malawi works alongside local church leaders to develop the knowledge, skills, and resources that can strengthen both their ministries and the communities they serve.

How Your Support Makes a Difference

Your gifts help:

  • Train pastors in sound theology and biblical leadership so they can faithfully preach the Gospel, disciple believers, and shepherd the churches entrusted to them.
  • Equip church plants with practical agricultural and entrepreneurial skills that strengthen families, create greater financial stability, and support sustainable local ministry.
  • Provide ongoing mentorship and accountability as pastors and congregations put their training into practice.
  • Supply biblical literature and ministry resources and aid translation efforts that strengthen local teaching, discipleship, and pastoral care.
  • Strengthen local churches and communities with practical tools and opportunities that help them make wise use of the resources God has provided.
  • Support the work of the Gospel in Malawi, trusting God to provide, sustain His Church, and bring lasting transformation through His Word.

Current Focus: Year-Round Farming Near Dzuwa ARPC Church

One of our current projects is helping develop farmland near Dzuwa ARPC Church in central Malawi.

At present, the farm depends on seasonal rainfall and is generally limited to one wet-season harvest. The next step is to install a solar-powered irrigation system using water from the nearby river. The project will include a solar pump, a holding and settling tank, and drip irrigation to make dry-season planting possible.

The estimated one-time cost is approximately $5,000.

This investment is about more than purchasing equipment. It can help create the conditions for more consistent food production, practical agricultural experience, and potential income—skills and resources that local leaders can continue building on long after the initial project is completed.

Stewardship & Accountability

We believe that every gift entrusted to Jars of Clay Foundation – Malawi should be used with wisdom, transparency, and care. Funds are directed toward ministry, training, and development initiatives in Malawi, with oversight and accountability provided by our Board of Directors. Our goal is not simply to meet immediate needs, but to invest resources in ways that strengthen local churches and communities for the long term.
